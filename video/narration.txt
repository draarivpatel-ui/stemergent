STEMergent is an offline weather forecast engine and heat safety policy planner built for school and community activity directors. Everything computes locally in the browser from labeled synthetic observation fixtures with zero network calls. On the Forecast Engine tab, you can switch between a synthetic summer heat wave and a synthetic winter cold snap to generate a deterministic twelve hour outlook, complete with National Weather Service heat index and wind chill math, a color temperature ribbon, and a written forecast discussion. On the Heat Policy Tracker tab, you log practice and event decisions against five heat risk tiers. Checking the forecast peak box pulls the highest heat index straight from the outlook, grades compliance automatically, flags unsafe full outdoor sessions, and records tier escalations ready for CSV or JSON export. Finally, the isolated Departments module manages departments, roles, and season calendars with blackout dates.
