About the Data

Every number here comes from the labeled synthetic fixture, never from a live weather service. The heat index and wind chill follow National Weather Service formulas, and the outlook uses a persistence model with a fixed diurnal adjustment, so results are deterministic and fully offline. The README documents exactly where a live api.weather.gov hourly feed would plug in.

Heat Policy Tracker

Log practice and event decisions against heat safety policy tiers across a season. The tracker checks each decision, records escalations when conditions worsen, and exports a summary for the season file.

Policy Tiers

Bands are modeled on National Weather Service heat risk categories. They are a planning aid, not official rule text.
